# Getting started

1. Enter a name or description, such as `DreamShaper 8` or `pictures of ginger cats in a rainy window`.
2. Use **Search areas** to focus the search and **Filter & sort** to refine it.
3. Select **Compare** on up to three models, or **Build a free report** to connect results across areas.

When the developer preview says **Exact title ready**, you can open that model version while RasterRaid checks for more results.

If a search finds few results, choose **Check model filters** or **Check media filters**. The current development build can show a sample that needs two settings to change. Review both settings and choose **Apply both changes**. **Back to original filters** restores the earlier search.

In the development build, try “Find SDXL LoRAs for Linocut Relief Print by @Booyaka in SafeTensor.” Review the search words and settings on **Search models with these filters**, then select it to apply them. Enter in the search box uses the complete text as written.

After a **Videos** search, use **Find a scene** to compare visible frames with a description. Play near a matched time or get a free quote for a Deep Search frame review. If the browser can only read the posters, RasterRaid ranks those and links to the clips. The description stays in your browser.

Choose **More ways to explore → Find a sound by description** after a **Videos** or **Audio & music** search. Describe what you want to hear in English. You can match a second sound in the same moment or prefer to avoid another. RasterRaid checks the first 30 seconds of up to four loaded public clips. The first check downloads about 42 MB and uses 0 Buzz.

Choose **Find spoken words** to check English speech in the same sample. Play near a match to confirm it by ear. The speech model downloads about 59 MB. Both checks run on your device.

Choose **Research this clip** to see live estimates for that recording. In Videos, **Review at playback position** uses the time you choose. You can also **Check recorded settings** for free on clips with published video metadata. In **Audio & music**, you can price a transcript and sound classification together. Your spoken-word query stays in the current tab.

Use **Add a picture** for visual comparisons. A public Civitai link opens its exact item. **Check recorded settings** shows the published references for a picture or clip. Paste two public media links to compare their settings.

Open the published app with an eligible Civitai account on the same domain. [Civitai.com](https://civitai.com/apps/run/rasterraid) · [Civitai.red](https://civitai.red/apps/run/rasterraid)

The developer preview requires the app author account. [Full guide](https://chriscollins500.github.io/rasterraid/guide/) · [`.com` preview](https://civitai.com/apps/dev/rasterraid) · [`.red` preview](https://civitai.red/apps/dev/rasterraid)
