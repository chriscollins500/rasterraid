# Getting started

1. Enter a name or description, such as `DreamShaper 8` or `pictures of ginger cats in a rainy window`.
2. Use **Search areas** to focus the search and **Filter & sort** to refine it.
3. Select **Compare** on up to three models, or **Build a free report** to connect results across areas.

When the developer preview says **Exact title ready**, you can open that model version while RasterRaid checks for more results.

After a **Videos** search, use **Find a scene** to compare visible frames with a description. Play near a matched time or get a free quote for a Deep Search frame review. If the browser can only read the posters, RasterRaid ranks those and links to the clips. The description stays in your browser.

The next developer build adds **More ways to explore → Find spoken words** after a **Videos** or **Audio & music** search. It checks English speech in the first 30 seconds of up to four loaded public clips. Play near a match to confirm it by ear. The check uses 0 Buzz and downloads about 59 MB of model files when you start it.

Use **Add a picture** for visual comparisons. A public Civitai link opens its exact item. **Check recorded settings** shows the published references for a picture or clip. Paste two public media links to compare their settings.
