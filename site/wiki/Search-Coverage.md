# Search coverage

| Area | What RasterRaid can do |
| --- | --- |
| Models and workflows | Find exact versions, files, hashes and AIR addresses. Inspect eligible workflow files through Civitai. |
| Public media | Search pictures, video, audio and posts. Sample media and compare picture, motion and sound traits. |
| Reading and discovery | Find articles, comics, storylines, creators, public collections, tools, apps and AI services. |
| Events and markets | Find bounties and public references to events, Crucibles, leaderboards, services, auctions and prizes. Open live feeds and account pages on Civitai. |
| Account areas | Search eligible collections and published models, select creations through Civitai and open native Vault, downloads, notifications and Chat. |

The host and the viewer’s account determine which results can appear. The full coverage page lists search and handoff behavior by area. [Filterable coverage list](https://chriscollins500.github.io/rasterraid/coverage/)
