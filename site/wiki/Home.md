# RasterRaid

RasterRaid runs inside Civitai. Search by name, description, picture or exact link, then compare the results or follow their sources.

- [Getting started](Getting-Started)
- [Search coverage](Search-Coverage)
- [Deep Search and Buzz](Deep-Search-and-Buzz)

Civitai serves RasterRaid 0.0.4 on both domains. [Civitai.com](https://civitai.com/apps/run/rasterraid) · [Civitai.red](https://civitai.red/apps/run/rasterraid)

The 0.0.11 developer prerelease finds sounds by description in loaded public clips. Listen near a matched moment and check live Deep Search prices for that clip.
