# RasterRaid

RasterRaid runs inside Civitai. Search by name, description, picture or exact link, then compare the results or follow their sources.

- [Getting started](Getting-Started)

- [Deep Search and Buzz](Deep-Search-and-Buzz)

RasterRaid 0.0.12 is live on both domains for accounts with Civitai Apps access. [Civitai.com](https://civitai.com/apps/run/rasterraid) · [Civitai.red](https://civitai.red/apps/run/rasterraid)

The 0.0.12 developer prerelease matches pictures to video moments, reads English speech from a matched sample, and shows private Workspace usage.
