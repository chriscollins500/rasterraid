# RasterRaid

RasterRaid runs inside Civitai. Search by name, description, picture or exact link, then compare the results or follow their sources.

The 0.0.11 developer prerelease finds sounds by description in loaded public clips. Listen near a matched moment and check live Deep Search prices for that clip. Civitai is reviewing the earlier 0.0.4 submission.
