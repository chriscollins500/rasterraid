# Workspace and privacy

Save searches and exact model picks in Workspace. **Files & setup** reads selected ComfyUI graphs, model files and supported metadata exports on the device. Catalog checks can use extracted exact IDs, model filenames or hashes. Report exports and backups remain files you control.

Signed-in viewers can use **Find my public work** in 0.0.6 to browse recent public pictures, clips and verified posts. Reopen a saved public search to check changes in its results.

In the development build, choose **Check and finish save** when a workspace change is still awaiting confirmation. The same action is checked against your private Civitai document.

Private collection access is separate from the AI permission. A published app can store your workspace settings and run receipts in Civitai’s private document store. Use **Security & Apps → App storage** in Civitai account settings to manage production app data.
