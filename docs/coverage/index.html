What RasterRaid can search

Both domains have been checked with the current developer build. The catalog you see depends on your Civitai account, browsing settings and the host’s available routes. For some areas RasterRaid finds the relevant listing, then opens Civitai’s full feed.

Models and media

Models, LoRAs and checkpoints
Search in RasterRaid

Filter by Civitai model family, type, creator and download access. Inspect exact releases and example pictures.

Workflows and files
Search and open on Civitai

Resolve exact versions, files, AIR addresses and hashes. Ask Civitai to read an eligible workflow graph or open the release.

Gallery images
Search in RasterRaid

Search by description, tag, creator, exact release, link or visual reference. Browse the returned pictures.

Videos
Search in RasterRaid

Play eligible clips and compare short sections by appearance, motion or sound.

Audio and music
Search in RasterRaid

Find audio models and playable public examples. Compare eligible excerpts by acoustic traits.

3D assets
Search and open on Civitai

Find public 3D collections. Individual assets and interactive previews open in the Civitai 3D gallery.

Posts
Search in RasterRaid

Find published posts and show eligible picture previews.

Tags and topics
Search and open on Civitai

Find catalog tags, sample topic pictures and open the broader native feeds.
