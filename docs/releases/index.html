0.0.4 developer audit

The November 24 audit exercised 39 search areas on each domain, including picture previews, video and music playback, free reports, workspace imports, live estimates, and older workflow receipts.

  • Search pauses for review when Civitai retires a selected sort or time period as you apply filters.
  • The free comparison, search-area, and shortcut dialogs close with keyboard or pointer controls inside Civitai’s frame.
  • Deep Search uses the selected previews for image descriptions and visual ranking. Audio reports extract the soundtrack of a video before describing it.
